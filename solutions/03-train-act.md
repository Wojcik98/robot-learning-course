# Lab 3 exercise guide

The changed variant must select the first 20 IDs from the frozen bowl-plate training list. Its 10 validation episode IDs and 20 development state IDs must match the reference. The run-specific split and statistics hash will differ from the 10- and 39-episode variants. Check `variants/demos20_chunk20/resolved/data/episodes.yaml` and `stats/bowl_plate.json` in your new output.

Compare success counts with all 20 attempts in each denominator. Keep any policy failure and infrastructure error visible. A lower loss measures how well the model fits its training batches; it does not directly measure whether its actions complete the task after a simulator reset. The demonstration sets are nested, so the 20-episode model has more unique examples and each example may be seen fewer times under the fixed 200-step budget.

One seed gives no training-seed variation. The 20 development starts can reveal a large change, but a small count difference may be uncertain. The source demonstrations may overlap the simulator starts. This exercise supports a practical recipe choice for this fixed task; it does not prove new-placement generalization. Final states remain closed until the frozen comparison.

The seeded reference scored **10/20** for 10 episodes with a 20-action chunk, **18/20** for 39 episodes with a 20-action chunk, and **11/20** for 39 episodes with a 10-action chunk. Each used 200 training steps and one training seed. The simulator used an explicit reset seed for each state. In the first pair, the 10-episode policy alone succeeded on two states, while the 39-episode policy alone succeeded on ten. The paired difference was 0.40 in favor of 39 episodes; the exact McNemar value was 0.039. In the chunk pair, the 20-action policy alone succeeded on eight states and the 10-action policy alone on one; its exact value was 0.039. These are development checks after one training seed. They do not establish a stable result across training seeds or new states. The [reference record](../artifacts/reference/act/lab3_suite.json) keeps the split and checkpoint hashes.
